The challenge

Why this matters.

Manual field monitoring is slow, costly, and increasingly ineffective against growing threats.

Crop losses from pest animals

Agricultural fields suffer billions in annual losses from wild boar, monkeys, deer, and rodent intrusions that go undetected until the damage is done.

Endangered species at risk

Poaching and habitat intrusion continue to threaten wildlife populations. Delayed detection often means irreversible harm.

Expensive manual patrols

Human patrol operations are resource-intensive, limited in coverage, and expose personnel to environmental hazards and fatigue.
